## Technical and security reports

For non-sensitive bugs, include the WhatsBibz and Node.js versions and reproduction steps in [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/XbibzOfficial777/whatsbibz/issues). For vulnerabilities or sensitive information, use the email above or [GitHub Security](https://github.com/XbibzOfficial777/whatsbibz/security/advisories/new) privately. Do not share pairing codes, tokens, or session files. See the [security policy](/en/security).
